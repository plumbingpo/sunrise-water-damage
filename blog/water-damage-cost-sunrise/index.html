How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Sunrise?

Home / Blog / How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Sunrise?

The Short Answer

Most water damage jobs in Sunrise fall somewhere between $1,500 and $10,000 or more. With clean water and a small area, the total can stay below about $1,500. Major flooding or a sewage backup that needs rebuilding can run well above $10,000.

Those numbers describe what is typical nationally; they are not a quote. What labor and materials cost in Sunrise, FL will shift the total higher or lower. An exact price requires someone to inspect the home in person and take moisture readings.

Pricing by Category of Water

The first thing a restoration estimate looks at is what kind of water it is. Contamination level places every loss in one of three industry categories. As contamination rises, so does the need for cleaning, tear-out of materials and protective equipment.

Nationally, the category puts cleanup at about $3 to $7.50 for each square foot. Typical figures look like this.

CategoryWhat it isTypical cost per sq ft
Category 1 (clean water)Supply lines, faucets, water heater tanksRoughly $3 to $4
Category 2 (gray water)Failed sump pumps, discharge from a dishwasher or washing machineRoughly $4 to $6.50
Category 3 (black water)Storm surge, flooding rivers, sewageRoughly $7 to $7.50

A loss does not always stay in one category. Leave clean water standing a couple of days and bacteria grow, so it gets handled as black or gray water. Putting things off raises the price for this reason, among others.

What Drives the Price

An identical pipe break in two houses can lead to bills that are far apart. When a job is priced, the contractor considers the following.

  • Area affected. More wet square footage means more labor, more equipment and more days on site.
  • Water category. Jobs with clean water cost the least. Sewage is the most expensive.
  • How long it sat. Given time, water works its way into wall cavities and subfloors. A leak that runs all weekend while you are gone costs far more than one caught in an hour.
  • Materials. Tile and concrete dry well. Special drying or outright replacement is common for drywall, hardwood, insulation and carpet pad.
  • Mold. Existing growth means remediation work, including containment and filtered air, becomes part of the job.
  • Reconstruction. Drying is one cost. Afterward comes a second cost for new flooring, drywall, paint and cabinets, which on larger jobs often exceeds the first.

Estimates usually cover a pair of phases, extraction of the water and then drying of the structure. Drying usually takes 3 to 5 days.

A daily rate is common for dehumidifiers and air movers. A home that dries in three days costs less than one that takes six.

Price Ranges by Type of Job

Below are the jobs our team handles most in Sunrise, with the national range that is typical for each. They are a place to begin, and an actual price will differ.

  • Burst pipe. Often $1,500 to $5,000 for cleanup and drying, plus the burst pipe repair itself. Expect more if it ran for hours or soaked more than one floor.
  • Appliance leak. Found early, a leak from a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine is often $1,000 to $4,000. Slow leaks under cabinets cost more because of hidden damage.
  • Basement flood. Typically $2,000 to $10,000 or more, depending on depth, the water source and whether the space is finished. Finished basements land near the top of the range for basement water removal.
  • Sewage backup. Many jobs cost $3,000 to $10,000, some more. Sewage cleanup is Category 3 work, so porous materials are removed and everything is disinfected.
  • Roof leak. Interior work on wet ceilings, walls and insulation often comes to $1,000 to $5,000, and the roof fix is extra.

Will Insurance Pay for Water Damage in Sunrise?

It depends on the cause and on your policy. A typical homeowners policy often covers accidental events that happen suddenly, for example a pipe bursting or a supply line giving out with no warning.

Gradual leaks are usually not covered. An insurer may treat a fitting that dripped for months as a maintenance problem. Outside flooding, for instance from storm surge or a river that rises, is usually not covered without separate flood insurance. For sewer backup, a dedicated add-on is often required.

Do not guess: look at your policy documents or ask the agent who sold them. Remember to consider your deductible. On a small job, the repair may cost less than the deductible, and a claim may not make sense.

Ways to Lower the Cost

Most of the cost of water damage is decided in the first day or two. A few simple steps make a real difference.

  • Act fast. Water left in place goes on spreading. More material gets wet with each passing hour.
  • Shut off the water. Learn the location of the main valve ahead of time, and turn it off the moment a leak shows up.
  • Document everything. Shoot video and pictures first, while everything is still in place and nothing has been tossed. Hold on to all receipts.
  • Call a pro before mold starts. Within 24 to 48 hours, mold growth can get underway. Once it does, the job gets longer and more expensive.
  • Move what you can. Get rugs, furniture and boxes off wet floors if it is safe.

If the power has not been cut, never wade into standing water, and avoid any water that could carry sewage. The risk of being shocked or getting sick outweighs anything you might save. If the water came from outside, leave flood cleanup to a crew with the right protective gear.

Getting a Real Number

A national range helps with budgeting, yet no house is the average house. Only a moisture meter inspection reveals where the water went and what drying and repairs will be needed.

If you need water damage restoration in Sunrise, FL, the best time to ask for that inspection is now, while the damage is still small.
