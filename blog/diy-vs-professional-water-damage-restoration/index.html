Should You DIY Water Damage Cleanup in Sunrise, FL

Home / Blog / Should You DIY Water Damage Cleanup in Sunrise, FL

When water damage strikes your Sunrise, FL home, one of the first decisions is whether to handle the cleanup yourself or call a professional restoration company. The instinct to save money by doing it yourself is understandable, but most water damage is far more complex than it appears on the surface. Below we explain which jobs a homeowner can reasonably take on and which ones need a professional crew.

Jobs You May Be Able to Handle Yourself

A minor spill that stays in one spot can sometimes be handled on your own. Say a sink overflows with clean water, wets a hard, non-porous floor over an area smaller than about 10 square feet, and is cleaned up inside of an hour: that is probably a job for you. Likewise, you most likely can skip the professional when a minor spill on a tile or vinyl floor is wiped up at once and dried with a dehumidifier and fans. Four things have to be true: clean water, a small area, immediate cleanup, and porous materials that were not significantly affected.

Situations That Need a Professional

In practice, the majority of water losses need a professional restoration crew. Pick up the phone immediately when more than 10 square feet got wet, the water sat longer than a few hours, carpet, drywall, or another porous material was soaked, gray or black water is involved, moisture could be inside walls or the structure, the leak cannot be found or shut off, or a musty smell hints that mold has started.

What DIY Cleanup Can Really Cost

We regularly meet Sunrise homeowners whose attempt to save money on cleanup left them with a bigger bill than professional work. Start with drying that stops too early: mold follows in a matter of days, and remediation runs $1,500 to $6,000 or more. Damaged materials that are not properly dried deteriorate further, increasing replacement costs. Renting fans, dehumidifiers, and an extractor gets expensive fast, and the results still fall short of professional work. Improper cleanup documentation may reduce your insurance payout. Finally, every day lost to a cleanup that is not working pushes real restoration back and lets the damage grow.

Why Professional Restoration Works Better

A professional crew brings things a homeowner cannot: extraction from a truck-mounted unit, hundreds of times faster at removing water, commercial dryers able to reach structural materials and the cavities inside walls, hidden moisture found by thermal imaging, readings that confirm drying is complete, an antimicrobial treatment to help keep mold from growing, full records to back up an insurance claim, and confidence that the work was done properly.

Anything more than a quick cleanup of clean water from hard flooring is a different story, and professional work is then the most cost-effective route back to normal.
