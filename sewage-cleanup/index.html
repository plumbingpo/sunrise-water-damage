Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Sunrise, Sunrise Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
