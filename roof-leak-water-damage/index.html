Roof Leak Water Damage in Sunrise: What to Know

A roof leak rarely starts where the stain appears. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. Our team maps everything that got wet, then dries it.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

First Five Minutes

Your First Steps

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Photograph the DamageTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.

Cost

The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Rafters and Decking

Wood in the roof structure that is soaked over and over darkens, swells and softens. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Hidden Attic Mold

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. It may take hold in 24 to 48 hours and work its way over the underside of the sheathing.

Attic Insulation

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Drying is sometimes possible with fiberglass. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Ceiling Drywall

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. Seams split, the tape lets go, and a heavy enough section can drop with little warning.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Map Where Water Went

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Cover and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. We also cover floors and contents under the damaged ceiling.

3

Tear Out and Dry

We pull out insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, then run air movers and dehumidifiers in the attic and the rooms below, checking moisture readings until the framing is dry.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
