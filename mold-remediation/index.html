Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. At Sunrise Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Sunrise homes and commercial properties.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. Those findings shape a plan written for your situation instead of a generic one. Throughout the project we maintain negative air pressure containment, run HEPA air scrubbers and follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. Afterward we verify the results, with clearance air testing if you request it, to confirm the property is safe to occupy.

The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

This is not a job to take on yourself. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs You Need Mold Remediation in Sunrise

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Use mold-resistant drywall and paint in moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and basements
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Give the crawl space proper ventilation, and think about adding a vapor barrier over the soil
  • Keep gutters clean so rainwater does not seep in along the foundation
