How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Sunrise

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
